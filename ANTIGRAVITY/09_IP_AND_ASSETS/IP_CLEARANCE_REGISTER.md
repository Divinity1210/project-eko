# PROJECT EKO — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY CLEARANCE REGISTER

> **Core Production Mandate**: Every geographic, architectural, automotive, audio, and visual asset in Project Eko must have an explicit legal basis before being committed to production builds. Scraped commercial logos, pirated music, and proprietary vehicle meshes are strictly prohibited.

---

## 1. Classification Taxonomy

* **Class A**: *Real Geography / Factual Location* — Factual, public geographical names, coordinates, and public domain landmarks (freely usable).
* **Class B**: *Generic Fictional Asset* — Original assets, fictional brands, original vehicle models, and custom textures created in-house.
* **Class C**: *Real-World Likeness Requiring Assessment* — Civic monuments, non-trademarked building facades, and public transit designs requiring legal copyright assessment.
* **Class D**: *Trademark / Licensed Commercial Asset* — Real corporate logos, brand placements, and commercial building names (permitted only under signed commercial contract).
* **Class E**: *Licensed Music / Audio* — Studio recordings, cleared sound effects, and licensed Afrobeats/Fuji tracks with verified master and publishing rights.
* **Class F**: *User-Generated Content (UGC)* — Player-created vehicle liveries, fashion items, and custom social events governed by platform Terms of Service.
* **Class G**: *Public / Open-Source Data* — Geodata and libraries used under open-source licenses (e.g., OpenStreetMap under ODbL, Three.js under MIT).

## 3. Strict Pre-Commit Verification Gate

Before any 3D model, sound file, or texture is merged into the monorepo:
1. **Provenance Check**: The author must document the source reference (e.g., original Blender file, field audio recording log).
2. **Trademark Inspection**: The 3D model must be inspected for accidental incorporation of real automotive badges, beverage logos, or fashion designer emblems.
3. **No Unlicensed Web Scrapes**: Zero textured meshes downloaded from public 3D asset aggregators containing non-commercial or proprietary licenses.
