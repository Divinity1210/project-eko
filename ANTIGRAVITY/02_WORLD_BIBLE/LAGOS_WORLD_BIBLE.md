## 1. World Scale Strategy & Coordinate System

### 1.1 The Playable Canvas
Lagos in Project Eko is not an unplayable 1:1 satellite dump of 1,171 km². It is a **curated, high-density topological compression** modeled on a 1:4 scale for major arterials and 1:2 scale for key urban pedestrian nodes.

* **World Coordinate Center $(0, 0, 0)$**: Tafawa Balewa Square (TBS) / CMS Interchange on Lagos Island.
* **Island Sector**: Negative $Z$ (South/East towards VI, Lekki, Eko Atlantic).
* **Mainland Sector**: Positive $Z$ (North across bridges towards Yaba, Surulere, Ikeja, Ojota, Mile 12).
* **Western Corridor**: Negative $X$ (West towards Iganmu, Mile 2, Alaba, Badagry).
* **Eastern / Lagoon Corridor**: Positive $X$ (East towards Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge, Ikoyi waterfront).

## 2. Definitive District Catalogue

### District 01: Marina / CMS / Lagos Island (The Commercial Core)
* **Real-World Reference**: Central Business District of Lagos, Marina Waterfront, Broad Street, CMS bus/ferry terminal, and Tafawa Balewa Square.
* **Game Coordinates**: Center $(0, 0, 0)$ to $(+800, 0, -600)$.
* **Primary Roads**: Broad Street, Marina Road, Inner Marina, Odunlami St, Catholic Mission St.
* **Landmarks**:
  * *Tafawa Balewa Square (TBS)*: Massive monument facade, ceremonial gates, horse statues.
  * *CMS Bus & Ferry Interchange*: High-intensity multi-modal terminal connecting road, rail, and water.
  * *Marina Blue Line Terminal*: Elevated modern rapid rail station over the waterfront.
  * *Old Colonial Commercial High-Rises*: High-density stone and concrete facades, bank headquarters.
* **Businesses & Workplaces**:
  * *Heritage Bank Towers*: High-paying white-collar corporate desk jobs (auditing, compliance).
  * *Marina Ferry Terminal Operations*: Water-taxi dispatch and ticket booking.
  * *Broad Street Photocopy & Legal Clerks*: Quick clerical missions and document delivery.
* **Transport Nodes**:
  * CMS Bus Park (BRT Lite Terminal, Danfo loading bays, yellow taxi stands).
  * Marina Blue Line Rail Station.
  * Marina Jetty (ferry connection to Ikorodu/Apapa).
* **NPC Archetypes & Behaviors**:
  * Suited bankers rushing with leather briefcases; aggressive Danfo conductors hanging out of doors shouting *"CMS! CMS! Straight!"*; roasted plantain (*boli*) street vendors; traffic wardens with yellow/orange reflective vests.
* **Activities & Missions**:
  * *Corporate Courier*: Deliver time-sensitive court documents from Marina to Alausa Secretariat in rush hour.
  * *Water-to-Wheel Commute*: Ferry passenger pickup from CMS jetty to Victoria Island financial district.
* **Commercial / Sponsor Opportunities**:
  * Premium banking and fintech sponsorships (branded bank branches and ATMs).
  * High-visibility digital rooftop billboards on Broad Street.

## 3. Major Express Bridges (Connecting Arterials)

The bridges of Lagos are legendary gameplay arenas with distinct weather physics and traffic dynamics:

1. **Third Mainland Bridge (11.8 km compressed to 3.2 km in-game)**:
   * Connects Mainland (Oworonshoki/Yaba) to Island (Marina/Ikoyi).
   * High-speed corridor (100–120 km/h).
   * Subject to violent lagoon crosswinds and torrential rain aquaplaning. Frequent broken-down vehicles and emergency tow missions.
2. **Carter Bridge**:
   * Connects Iddo/Oyingbo to Idumota on Lagos Island.
   * High density of pedestrian crossing, heavy Danfo lines, and tight stop-and-go driving.
3. **Eko Bridge (First Mainland Bridge)**:
   * High flyover connecting Surulere/Costain directly to Marina.
   * Smooth asphalt, sweeping curves, prime route for executive commutes.
4. **Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge**:
   * Iconic cable-stayed suspension bridge connecting Lekki Phase 1 to Ikoyi.
   * Toll booth gameplay, luxury vehicle cruising, pedestrian jogging lane.
